How to read the constellation

Pick a star.

  • A star is one record. A document, a piece of code, an automation, a concept or an agent role.
  • A thread is a relationship. It has a name and a direction, like depends on or supersedes. The moving spark shows which way it points.
  • A question draws a constellation. It lights a few stars, follows their threads outward, then keeps only what fits a small budget. That constellation is the context an AI agent receives.

Click any star or thread to open it. Every record in this sky was invented for the exhibit.
